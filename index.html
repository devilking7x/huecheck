HueCheck

Can real people actually read this colour combination?

Build a palette. HueCheck measures the real WCAG contrast ratio of every pair, shows how the palette looks under colour-vision deficiency, and lists the text/background combinations that actually pass. Everything runs in your browser — no server, no tracking, no data leaves the page.

3. Colour-vision-deficiency simulation

Each palette colour as seen with protanopia (no red cones), deuteranopia (no green cones), tritanopia (no blue cones) and achromatopsia (no colour at all). Simulation uses the Machado et al. (2009) severity-1.0 matrices in linear sRGB — an approximation, not a medical test (see Honest limits).

Honest limits

  • WCAG contrast ratios are computed exactly from the WCAG 2.x formula — but a passing ratio does not guarantee readability for every person. Font choice, size, weight, spacing and layout all matter, and this tool does not check them.
  • CVD simulation is an approximation (Machado model, severity 1.0 — full loss of one cone type). Most real colour-vision deficiency is milder (anomalous trichromacy), so real shifts are usually smaller. It is a design check, not a medical test.
  • The tool checks colours only — not fonts, sizes, layouts, images, or motion.
  • Ratios are rounded to 2 decimals for display; verdicts use the unrounded value.
